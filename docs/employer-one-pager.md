# Capital Mastery for Employers

## Make new finance talent productive sooner

Capital Mastery is **Finance Workforce Readiness Infrastructure** for interns, analysts, new hires and targeted development cohorts. It sits beside an employer's normal HR onboarding and focuses on the question traditional onboarding usually cannot answer: **Can this person perform the role-specific finance work at the required standard?**

### What employers assign

- 16 finance career pathways.
- Foundations or full Professional Readiness tracks.
- Baseline diagnostics with 0% credential weight.
- Teach-first technical and professional toolkits.
- Applied Work in role-format workspaces.
- Secure Role Labs with revisions and changing information.
- Professional Readiness Finals.
- Five credential levels per career, plus cross-career Academy achievements.

### What managers see

- learner progress stage versus actual readiness;
- evidence coverage;
- competency-level scores and critical floors;
- Role Lab score and revision history;
- final-assessment result;
- prioritized Manager Attention Queue;
- audited manager coaching/review notes;
- deadline and readiness notifications;
- CSV and evidence JSON exports.

### Capital Mastery Standard + Firm Layer

The standardized credential requirements stay protected. A firm can add its own terminology, expectations, resources, cases, manager notes and exercises, reorder those additions, and hide/archive/restore them. Employer permanent delete is intentionally unavailable; content is versioned.

### Trust model

Firebase Authentication handles identity. The Capital Mastery Cloudflare Worker + D1 database are authoritative for tenant roles, official grading, prerequisites, competency evidence, enterprise progress and credentials. Answer keys and grading rules stay server-side.

### Pilot suggestion

Start with one role, one cohort and one deadline. Compare baseline diagnostic evidence with post-Role-Lab/final readiness, inspect revision patterns, and ask managers whether the evidence changed where they spent coaching time.

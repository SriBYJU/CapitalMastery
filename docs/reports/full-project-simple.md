# Capital Mastery — Full Project Simple Report

## Overall result

Capital Mastery is now a working, production-deployed finance career-readiness platform suitable for publication, serious demonstrations and controlled employer pilots.

The platform includes 16 finance career pathways, two program depths, 80 five-level career credential definitions, realistic graded work, secure assessment review, verifiable evidence and an employer workspace. It is free for learners and employers.

## What a learner can do

- Choose a career and follow a guided path from foundations to applied work.
- Practice after learning rather than facing unexplained tests.
- Complete calculations, source analysis, written work and role-native simulations instead of an MCQ-only experience.
- Use one Continue action to return to the correct unfinished step.
- See locked prerequisites, preserve progress across devices and review past assessment answers.
- Retake failed work, while passed assessments remain permanently read-only.
- Earn and publicly verify evidence-backed credentials.

## What an employer can do

- Create a guided workspace, cohort and assignment.
- Invite learners through exact-email, role-protected flows.
- Assign Career Skills or Professional Readiness programs.
- Add versioned firm-specific material without overwriting the Capital Mastery Standard.
- Review evidence, revision behavior, competency signals and readiness reports.
- Use role-based access, audit history, notifications and safe CSV exports.

## Quality and assurance

The product has passed 88 current dependency-free static audit files, all-career/two-program/six-width browser coverage, a nineteen-suite live-primary browser matrix, hostile route/state tests, course continuity, account isolation, visual contrast, employer roles, a real disposable Firebase account lifecycle and production D1 checks. Phase 3 added CSP, SRI, stricter API inputs, keyboard dialogs, daily monitoring, bounded weekly resilience checks, recovery/incident documentation and a final first-paint repair that keeps passed-assessment Continue and Review behavior deterministic.

## Bottom line

Functionally, it is nearly production-grade for its current free-project scope and credible enough to take to a large firm for a demo or pilot discussion. The honest next step is real user and employer feedback—not pretending that internal engineering evidence equals SOC 2, legal approval, independent penetration testing or bank endorsement.

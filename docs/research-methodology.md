# Research Methodology

Capital Mastery uses a source-to-simulation approach:

**public source → documented professional task → learning objective → assessment → simulation evidence**

Sources represented in the build include U.S. Bureau of Labor Statistics, O*NET, SEC EDGAR, Federal Reserve FRED, CFA Institute, J.P. Morgan, Goldman Sachs, Morgan Stanley, Blackstone, BlackRock, Fidelity, Jane Street, Insight Partners, AFP, Forage, CFI, Wall Street Prep, Training The Street, LinkedIn credential documentation, 1EdTech Open Badges, Credential Engine and Firebase documentation.

Employer names are used to identify public research sources only. They do not imply sponsorship, affiliation or endorsement.

## Real vs simulated data

Exercises are designed to use one of two clear labels:

- **REAL DATA** — information derived from publicly available filings/datasets with provenance.
- **SIMULATED CASE** — fictional scenario/data designed to test competencies.

The product intentionally does not blur the two.

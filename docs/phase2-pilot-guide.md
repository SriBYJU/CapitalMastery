# Capital Mastery Phase 2 — Employer Pilot Guide

## Pilot objective

Test whether Capital Mastery can make pre-Day-1 finance preparation more standardized, measurable and manager-actionable without replacing a firm's HR onboarding or internal training.

## Recommended first pilot

Use one role-specific cohort of roughly 5–25 interns, analysts or new hires. Investment Banking is the deepest reference implementation, but Phase 2 supports all 16 careers through the same Standard 2.0 evidence architecture.

## Launch sequence

1. Create the employer workspace and complete the Interactive Launch Guide.
2. Create a cohort with Quick Assign.
3. Choose Foundations or Professional Readiness.
4. Review the entire learner curriculum before publishing.
5. Add only firm-specific material that improves Day-1 relevance.
6. Invite learners with the secure context-aware invitation flow.
7. Capture the assigned baseline diagnostic before professional evidence begins.
8. Monitor evidence coverage and progress, not just course completion.
9. Use the Manager Attention Queue for overdue work, repeated revisions and evidence-backed skill gaps.
10. Add manager review notes when coaching is needed.
11. Export the final readiness report and evidence JSON.
12. Compare baseline, final readiness, revisions, manager time and post-start performance using the firm's own KPIs.

## Suggested pilot success metrics

- completion rate by deadline;
- median baseline diagnostic score;
- median final readiness score;
- median evidence coverage;
- critical competency floor attainment;
- Role Lab first-pass rate;
- Role Lab revision count;
- manager coaching flags per learner;
- employer-estimated time-to-productivity after start;
- manager confidence in new-hire readiness;
- new-hire confidence in role expectations.

## What not to claim

Do not treat Capital Mastery readiness as a hiring guarantee, job-performance guarantee, licensing result, investment recommendation or substitute for employer supervision. Public onboarding research shown in the app is context, not a promised Capital Mastery uplift.

## QA before inviting real learners

Use the Admin Demo/Test Lab to create synthetic cohorts, force learner states, inspect reports, test permissions and verify Firm Layer behavior without creating real Firebase learner accounts.

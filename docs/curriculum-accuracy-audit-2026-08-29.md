# Capital Mastery Phase 2 Curriculum Accuracy Audit

**Verification date:** August 29, 2026  
**Scope:** 16 finance-career pathways, professional-toolkit surfaces, Applied Work, secure assessments and Role Labs.  
**Standard:** Training should reflect the real decisions, work products, source discipline and review behavior of the role while avoiding employer-proprietary software, data or internal processes.

## Audit principles

1. **Teach before testing.** Advanced calculations or professional judgments must first appear in the learning/toolkit experience before they can become credential-gating evidence.
2. **Work products over trivia.** Role readiness should rely on models, workpapers, source files, calculations, recommendations and revision behavior—not MCQ completion alone.
3. **Current public role evidence.** Career tasks are triangulated against current employer programs/roles, professional bodies and public occupational sources.
4. **Synthetic cases only.** Employer names in research sources do not mean those firms use or endorse Capital Mastery. Role Labs use fictional companies, people and case data.
5. **No proprietary UI claims.** Workbenches are training representations of common workflows, not replicas of a firm's internal tools.

## Phase 2 changes driven by this audit

- Treasury Applied Work was rebuilt from generic text responses into cash/liquidity, funding, debt-maturity and FX/hedge workpapers.
- Non-IB professional assessments were upgraded from MCQ-heavy screens to mixed calculations, source tables, professional written responses and judgment items.
- Private Equity Toolkit/Applied Work now uses an LBO + Investment Committee workbench instead of the generic spreadsheet shell.
- Venture Capital now uses a market, unit-economics, cap-table and cohort workspace with a partner recommendation workflow.
- Corporate Development now uses a target, valuation, synergy, diligence and integration deal room with an executive recommendation workflow.
- Employer/learner progress views now read authoritative server evidence so UI completion cannot disagree with official assessment/credential state.

## Reverification rule

When a public employer role/program used as a benchmark changes or disappears, the curriculum does **not** automatically change. Instead, replace the source with another current authoritative source, compare the actual task model, and only change curriculum content when the underlying professional workflow has materially changed. This prevents trend-chasing and protects curriculum stability.

# Capital Mastery — Workforce Evidence Ledger

Capital Mastery uses public workforce research to explain the business problem it addresses. These findings are **context**, not Capital Mastery outcome claims or guarantees.

## Public findings used in product positioning

| Finding | How Capital Mastery uses it | Public source |
|---|---|---|
| Only 12% of employees strongly agree their organization does a great job onboarding new employees. | Explains the quality gap in the broader onboarding experience. | Microsoft New Employee Onboarding Hub, citing Gallup: https://techcommunity.microsoft.com/blog/spblog/the-new-employee-onboarding-hub-%E2%80%93-improving-the-new-hire-experience/1530083 |
| Systematic onboarding can bring new employees up to speed 50% faster. | Supports measuring time-to-productivity rather than treating onboarding as paperwork completion. | SHRM / HBR: https://www.shrm.org/topics-tools/news/talent-acquisition/7-ways-to-set-new-hire-success |
| Microsoft cites SHRM research that employees with a great onboarding experience are 69% more likely to stay for three years. | Supports the broader importance of a well-structured early employee experience. | Microsoft New Employee Onboarding Hub: same source above. |
| Microsoft cites Gallup research that a negative onboarding experience makes a new employee twice as likely to look for other opportunities within six months. | Supports addressing readiness and clarity early instead of waiting for Day 1 problems. | Microsoft New Employee Onboarding Hub: same source above. |
| SHRM recommends time-to-productivity as a core onboarding metric. | Capital Mastery employer reporting is designed around measurable readiness evidence rather than clicks. | SHRM, Measuring Success: https://www.shrm.org/topics-tools/topics/onboarding/measuring-success |

## Claim boundary

Capital Mastery does **not** claim that using the platform causes the percentages above, guarantees retention, produces a specific productivity improvement, or creates a universal return on investment. The employer Ramp-Value Model is intentionally a scenario calculator using employer-entered assumptions.

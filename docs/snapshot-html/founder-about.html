ABOUT THE FOUNDER

Shriyan Avadhanula

Founder, Capital Mastery

Shriyan created Capital Mastery as a student-first way to understand finance careers without separating “learning about the job” from “learning how to do the job.” The platform combines concise teaching, public-data exercises, professional work products, graded simulations and transparent credential standards.

The goal is simple: a learner should enter an internship, student finance organization or future analyst training with a practical foundation that goes far beyond memorized interview definitions.

Capital Mastery is an independent educational project. Employer names referenced in research sections are public sources, not endorsements.

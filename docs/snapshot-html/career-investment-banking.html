THE JOB

Investment Banking Analyst

Learn how junior bankers analyze companies, value businesses, build transaction materials and support live M&A execution.

Purpose
Advise corporate and sponsor clients on major strategic and financing transactions.

Who you work with
Corporations, financial sponsors, boards and other institutional clients.

How the business earns revenue
Advisory and underwriting fees tied to transactions and capital raising.

FINAL JOB SIMULATION

Project Northstar

You are a first-year Investment Banking Analyst on an M&A team advising Northstar Technologies on a potential acquisition of Orion Systems.

Your work

  1. Review the client brief and identify the decision that must be made.
  2. Research Northstar and Orion using provided public-company style materials.
  3. Select and defend a comparable-company peer set.
  4. Complete valuation outputs and reconcile DCF, comps and precedents.
  5. Analyze purchase price, financing and synergy assumptions.
  6. Prepare an executive recommendation and respond to associate comments.

What can change

Finance work is not static. The case introduces new information after you start.

  • Management lowers Orion revenue guidance midway through the process.
  • One comparable company announces a major acquisition and becomes less comparable.

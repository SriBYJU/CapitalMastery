# Capital Mastery — 15-Minute Employer Demo Playbook

## 0:00–2:00 — The problem

Show the public onboarding evidence section. Explain the distinction between normal HR onboarding and finance-role readiness. Do not claim Capital Mastery causes the external research outcomes.

## 2:00–4:00 — Command Center

Open a synthetic Mixed Cohort. Point out started, ready, evidence coverage and the Manager Attention Queue. Explain why completion and readiness are separated.

## 4:00–6:00 — Curriculum + Firm Layer

Show the protected Capital Mastery Standard, then add/edit/reorder a Firm Layer item. Open version history. Attempt to hide required standard content and show the server-side denial.

## 6:00–10:00 — Learner work

Open a role-specific Professional Toolkit and Applied Work surface, then a Role Lab. Show changing information, secure grading and the revision loop.

## 10:00–12:00 — Readiness evidence

Open an employer report, drill into a learner, add a manager review note, then export CSV/evidence JSON.

## 12:00–13:30 — Credentials

Show the five-level stack and an Academy roll-up. Open public verification and explain the evidence boundary.

## 13:30–15:00 — Trust + pilot

Show Team & Roles, Audit Log, Trust Center and Demo/Test Lab. Offer a one-role pilot with success metrics defined by the firm before launch.

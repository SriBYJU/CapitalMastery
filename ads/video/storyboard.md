# Capital Mastery Video Ad Storyboard

## After Effects-style dynamic campaign

The dynamic campaign is a separate motion-graphics system rather than a screenshot slideshow. It uses kinetic type, animated interface components, formula entry, live model changes, review comments, readiness bars, evidence connections and audit events. Two 15-second vertical masters are delivered for learner and employer audiences, with silent and soothing female-voiceover versions.

The visual reference is modern app/UI motion design: fast but controlled scene choreography, clean easing, restrained brand color, information appearing through interaction and a decisive final CTA. The production stays original to Capital Mastery and does not reproduce another creator's branded artwork.

## 15–17 second vertical cut

1. **Hook — 0:00–0:02**  
   Finance training shouldn't stop at course completion.
2. **Role learning — 0:02–0:04**  
   Learn the actual job and the technical workflow.
3. **Applied work — 0:04–0:06**  
   Build real work products, calculations and decision takeaways.
4. **Secure assessment — 0:06–0:08**  
   Apply the material with source files and server-side grading.
5. **Revision behavior — 0:08–0:10**  
   Weak work can be rejected. Revise before moving on.
6. **Employer evidence — 0:10–0:12**  
   Managers see readiness, evidence, Role Lab performance and revision history.
7. **Free — 0:12–0:14**  
   16 finance careers. Free for learners. Free for employers.
8. **Brand / CTA — 0:14–end**  
   Capital Mastery — Learn it. Practice it. Prove it. Start free.

## 30-second cut

Use the same sequence with 3–4 seconds per product screen and add: the Firm Layer, verified credentials, manager evidence review, learner feedback notification, and Trust Center. The stronger B2B ending is: **"Prepare finance talent before Day 1—without adding another seat fee."**

## Delivered campaign cuts

### Learner work — 18.6 seconds

1. Don't just finish a finance course. Finish the work.
2. Learn inside the workflow.
3. Use source data. Calculate. Explain.
4. Handle the live update.
5. Get reviewed. Revise. Improve.
6. Prove what you can do.
7. 16 finance careers. Start free.

### Employer readiness — 18.6 seconds

1. Training completed. Can the analyst do the work?
2. Assign the exact readiness path.
3. Review the work—not a self-report.
4. Make feedback part of training.
5. See readiness by competency.
6. Keep the audit trail.
7. Prepare finance talent before Day 1.

## Creative rules

Use production UI or clearly labeled synthetic case data. Keep the product independent: never place a bank/firm logo in a way that implies endorsement or customer status. Avoid unverified performance or hiring claims. Keep `$0`, `free for learners`, and `free for employers` prominent because those are current product terms, not a limited trial.
